Who We Serve Best

Pointer Creek is not designed to be everything to everyone. As your fiduciary, we work best with clients who value honesty, thoughtful planning, and a long-term partnership — not shortcuts, hype, or performance theater. Our best relationships share a handful of traits that have far more to do with mindset than with age or profession.

Ideal Clients & Where We Fit

You’re interviewing us as much as we’re interviewing you — fit matters, a lot. A fiduciary partnership with us fits best when:

Where we fit

  • You want the truth — even when it isn’t what you were hoping to hear. As your fiduciary, we won’t inflate assumptions or sugarcoat reality just to make a plan look better. You’d rather hear an honest “no” or “not yet” than a comfortable answer that won’t hold up.
  • You’re a busy delegator — for you, delegation is a lifestyle. You could manage much of this yourself, but you’d rather hand the details to a fiduciary you trust and spend your time and energy elsewhere.
  • You want clarity, not jargon. No “strategy of the month.” You want the complexity handled behind the scenes and the conclusions and tradeoffs explained in plain English.
  • You’re rebuilding trust after a bad experience. After advisor fatigue or product-churn, you want someone whose only agenda is your outcome.
  • You think in decades, not quarters. You value patience, discipline, and follow-through — a long-term fiduciary thinking partner who keeps the people who come after you in mind.

If your goal would require the alignment of the sun, the moon, the stars, and a Powerball ticket, we’ll tell you — with a smile — then build a fiduciary plan grounded in what actually works.

Life Situations We Work Best With

While we serve a diverse range of entities, trusts, families, and individual clients — including private businesses, family limited partnerships (FLPs), private foundations, non-profits, 501(c) organizations, retirement plans, and endowments — there are several change situations where Pointer Creek consistently delivers exceptional value. These aren’t “client types.” They are inflection points where thoughtful, multidisciplinary planning matters.

Newly Formed Entities & Startup Architecture

Entity choice, tax setup, and retirement structure — set right on day one.

Read more →

We help founders and new business owners make early decisions that will influence their financial and tax lives for decades. This includes:

  • evaluating entity types (LLC, S-corp, C-corp, partnership, etc.),
  • understanding tax classifications and long-term implications,
  • assembling the right professional team (legal, tax, accounting, payroll, benefits),
  • establishing retirement plans that use tax-deferred or tax-free dollars,
  • designing early-stage compensation and ownership structures,
  • implementing tax-managed strategies from day one.

Wise decisions on day one can save extraordinary amounts of money, time, and stress later. We help founders start strong.

The Retirement Red Zone

“What got you here won’t get you there.” When saving becomes spending.

Read more →

We work extremely well with individuals and couples approaching an impending retirement date — the retirement red zone. This season often drives people to seek a new advisor because, as we tell clients:

“What got you here, won’t get you there.”

We say this because saving for retirement is very different from spending in retirement. A portfolio built for growth or self-directed accumulation may not be structured to deliver protected, tax-efficient, dependable income for decades.

We help clients transition from accumulation to distribution by analyzing:

  • asset allocation and asset location,
  • risk capacity vs. risk tolerance,
  • sequence-of-return risk,
  • Social Security interactions,
  • pension timing and optimization,
  • tax brackets and withdrawal strategies,
  • healthcare and long-term care planning,
  • the emotional and lifestyle adjustments that come with retirement.

We help clients enter retirement not just confident — but fully prepared.

Where We’re Not a Fit

Honesty runs both ways. Fiduciary respect means being just as clear about who we can’t serve well:

Where we’re not a fit

  • You’re looking for hot stock tips, market predictions, or something exciting to talk about on the golf course. That’s not how we manage serious money.
  • You want an advisor to tell you what you want to hear. Sometimes good fiduciary advice means saying that a goal, assumption, or strategy simply doesn’t work.
  • You need daily lifestyle policing or spending guardrails. If you need someone to stop the bottle service or put a hold on chartering the G650 to the beach, there are firms built for that. We’re not one of them.
  • Mutual respect isn’t negotiable here. A large portfolio doesn’t excuse treating our team—or the people around you—poorly. Fit matters more than account size.
