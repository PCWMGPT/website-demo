Who Pointer Creek Serves Best

Fiduciary financial planning for a specific kind of client.

Pointer Creek Wealth Management is not built to be everyone’s advisor. We’re built for people who want honest fiduciary advice, thorough planning, and a long-term relationship — not a sales pitch, a hot tip, or a rotating cast of “advisors.”

Ideal Client Traits: Who Fits at Pointer Creek

You want the truth over the sale

You’d rather hear “that’s a bad idea, and here’s why” than be agreed with. This single trait predicts more good outcomes than any asset level we’ve ever seen.

You think in decades

Quarterly performance is not the scoreboard you’re actually playing on.

You accept evidence over opinion

Low-fee strategies grounded entirely in academic research. “We don’t guess. We model.” See fiduciary wealth management.

You care about the people who come after you

If your planning question includes someone other than yourself, you’re already thinking the way we do. See trust & estate planning.

You want a partner, not a vendor

Someone who’ll still pick up the phone in fifteen years and remembers the reasoning behind the decision you made in 2019.

Asset Profile & Investment Minimum, Stated Plainly

The managed-account minimum, stated plainly

$500,000+ in net investible assets for a new fiduciary managed account relationship. We’d rather you read that here than discover it in a fourth meeting.

Planning is scoped to complexity, not size

Some of the most consequential work we do is for people whose decisions are enormous even when their balances are ordinary.

Complexity matters more than size

A large portfolio in three index funds is a simpler problem than a modest one involving an S-corp, a blended family, a pension with a WEP offset, and a cabin nobody has retitled since 1978.

Where we’re genuinely not the right fit

If you want a manager who’ll beat an index on demand, we’ll disappoint you — and we’d rather do that in the first hour than the fifth year.
