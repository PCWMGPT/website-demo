Trust & Estate Planning

Most advisors stop after saying “here’s your trust.” We continue through implementation — wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, beneficiary reviews, and asset titling, then the follow-through most plans skip: updating deeds, changing ownership, funding the trust, legacy letters, and walking clients through what the documents actually say.

What We Handle

  • Wills & Trusts
  • Powers of Attorney & Healthcare Directives
  • Beneficiary Reviews & Asset Titling
  • Trust Funding & Deed Updates
  • Legacy Letters

We coordinate with your attorney; we do not draft legal documents. Cambridge does not offer tax or legal advice.
