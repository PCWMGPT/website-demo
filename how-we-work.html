Our Process

How we actually work.

Most advisory firms describe what they sell. Very few explain how they actually work. At Pointer Creek, process matters — not because we love complexity, but because thoughtful planning, accountability, and follow-through are what protect clients from mistakes, missed steps, and conflicts of interest. Here is exactly how our real process works, from the first conversation to long-term partnership.

Start With an Introductory Meeting

Most of our clients come to us by referral. We don’t buy leads, we don’t run ads, and we don’t sponsor billboards or golf outings — in fact, we routinely maintain a waiting list of people who’d like to become clients. Others find us through careful online research after searching specifically for fiduciary, CFP® practitioner, conflict-free advice. When they arrive, they often tell us:

“I can’t believe you actually publish your fees.” · “I can’t believe you’ll just charge me for the planning time I use, with no asset requirement.” · “I can’t believe you built me a plan and the answer wasn’t ‘roll everything over to us.’”

However you find us, everyone enters the same way: with an introductory meeting.

This isn’t a sales presentation; it’s a mutual interview. We use it to understand who you are, what you’re hoping we can help with, what success looks like six months or six decades from now, and how you prefer to make decisions. At the same time, you’re evaluating us. By the end, we commit to giving you three things: an honest read on mutual fit, an estimate of the time, cost, and complexity involved, and a clear outline of next steps — even if the right answer is pointing you toward someone else.

We’re candid about what we don’t do. If your goal is to have an advisor day-trade cryptocurrency and turn $10,000 into $10 million, we’re not that firm. If your goal is to build a durable plan, protect your family, and use money intentionally over time, we’re likely a strong match.

When you hire us for planning, we lead with planning and complete the full process before implementing any wealth- or risk-management solutions. We follow the CFP Board’s seven-step process — discovery, data gathering, analysis and modeling, recommendations, implementation, coordination, and ongoing monitoring. That part is standard for CFP® professionals. What isn’t standard is nearly 25 years of complex-planning experience from our lead planner, comfort working with everyone from newly married couples to companies valued well over $100 million, and the ability to act as a “CFO for hire” in high-need situations — while still maintaining quality for every other client.

“We don’t guess. We model.”

We’re transparent about capacity. At any given time we actively work on only ten financial plans; when one is completed, the next client on the waiting list moves into “active” status. This ensures every engagement gets real attention, we never rush analysis, and existing clients aren’t crowded out. A typical planning engagement runs four to eight weeks — the length depends mostly on how quickly you can complete your “homework” (statements, benefit details, Social Security estimates). The planning engine on our end is efficient; the natural pauses are in information-gathering.

We put unusual energy into documentation and follow-through, because that’s where many advisory relationships quietly fail. Meeting notes go through a secure, encrypted dictation and internal quality-control process, become structured tasks in our practice-management software, are verified by our operations manager, and are reviewed each morning at a firm-level meeting. A client with $10 million and a client with $1 million receive the same checklist-driven care.

We don’t leave your financial life to chance, inbox chaos, or someone’s sticky note.

Estate funding & “if you died tomorrow” testing. Many advisors — and even some attorneys — stop at “getting documents signed.” We don’t. We go asset by asset, from the knickknacks to the multi-million-dollar account, and check how each is owned, titled, and what happens at death or incapacity. We even run what we jokingly call a “paper assassination” exercise — a simulated walk-through of what would actually happen if you died tomorrow. This testing has helped clients avoid, collectively, millions in probate costs and years of headaches.

Deep-dive risk management analysis. With no allegiance to any insurer or product, our models test multiple carriers, structures, and stress scenarios — comparisons that would “wrap around the conference room.” Our loyalty is to the client outcome, not to any company.

Balancing wealth, life, and legacy. We’re not here to tell people to hoard every dollar. It’s not unusual for us to tell a client, “You can afford to spend some of this — on travel, experiences, generosity, or simply breathing room.” The point isn’t to die with the largest spreadsheet; it’s to live a life and leave a legacy that reflect your values.

As a completely independent, owner-controlled firm, we have no internal quotas for assets under management, no annual AUM growth targets, and no production goals tied to products or platforms. We avoid these because they introduce conflicts that pull attention away from true fiduciary advice. If you’ve ever seen Field of Dreams, it’s very much an “if you build it, they will come” mentality.

Our incentives are aligned with getting the planning right — not with gathering as many assets as possible.
