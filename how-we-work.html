Our Process

How we actually work.

Most advisory firms describe what they sell. Very few explain how they actually work. At Pointer Creek, process matters — not because we love complexity, but because thoughtful planning, accountability, and follow-through are what protect clients from mistakes, missed steps, and conflicts of interest. Below is exactly how our real process works, from the first conversation to long-term partnership.

We start with a conversation, not a pitch. As a fiduciary, CFP®-practitioner firm, our advice is conflict-free and our fees are transparent — we don’t work on quotas, and we don’t need to move your accounts to be helpful. The first step is simply understanding what’s going on and whether our depth is relevant to it. See Introductory Meeting.

The introductory meeting exists to answer one question honestly: are we a fit for each other? It’s where we do the self-selection work most firms avoid — we’ll tell you plainly if your situation isn’t one where our depth adds value, and point you somewhere better if that’s the right answer. No product is recommended, and no proposal is made before a plan exists.

Planning comes before any investment recommendation. We apply the CFP® process thoroughly — and then go beyond it — across retirement income, tax, estate, business, and risk. The plan tells us what the money needs to do; the investments are simply one tool for accomplishing it.

“We don’t guess. We model.”

Related: Financial Planning · Estate Planning · Investment Philosophy.

We stay small on purpose, which means we work with a limited number of relationships at a time and are honest about timelines. Thorough planning takes weeks to months, not days — we’d rather give you an accurate estimate than an attractive one. When capacity is full, serious prospects wait rather than receive rushed work.

Good advice shouldn’t depend on one person’s memory. We document decisions and the reasoning behind them, follow repeatable processes, and build in quality control so nothing quietly falls through the cracks. It’s the quiet part of the work — and a large part of why plans actually get implemented here rather than filed away.

Estate funding & “if you died tomorrow” testing. Documents are the easy half. We pressure-test what would actually happen — funding trusts, updating deeds, retitling assets — so the plan works when it’s needed, not just on paper.

Deep-dive risk management analysis. Not “do you have insurance,” but what would actually happen to the cash flow, the business, and the surviving spouse.

Balancing wealth, life, and legacy. We keep the whole picture in view — the people who come after you included — rather than optimizing a portfolio in isolation.

We believe asset-gathering quotas create conflicts. We chose a different model — small, specialized, CFP®-only, with no proprietary products and no pressure to move your money. When there’s nothing to sell you, advice can simply be advice.

“We believe asset-gathering quotas create conflicts. We chose a different model.”
