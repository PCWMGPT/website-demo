Welcome

Welcome to Pointer Creek Wealth Management, a financial solutions firm. We provide private wealth management and fee-based CFP® practitioner financial planning services to high-net-worth investors.

Why Pointer Creek?

Pointer Creek was founded on one belief: every client deserves honest, fiduciary financial advice from someone who has earned it.

Our CFP® professionals have met rigorous standards and are held to a fiduciary duty, meaning your interests always come first. We take your goals seriously and provide clear guidance across retirement planning, investment strategy, and long-term financial decisions.

We’ll show you options and solutions that were unseen before.

Fiduciary, Always

We are your fiduciary. Your interests always come first. We use the same strategies and investments we recommend and stand behind every decision we present.
