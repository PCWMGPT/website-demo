Ways to Work With Us

Planning comes first. Then you decide.

Every Pointer Creek relationship begins with planning — understanding what your money is intended to accomplish before recommending any strategy. From there, you choose what responsibilities to keep and what to delegate. Our services are priced separately, so you never pay for what you don’t need.

“What should we do?”

Start with a Fiduciary Planning Project — we analyze the situation, build the architecture, and provide the roadmap.

“How does the planning continue?”

Choose Ongoing Fiduciary Planning — we proactively monitor, coordinate, and advance the plan.

“Who will manage the portfolio?”

Choose Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management — we assume ongoing fiduciary responsibility for the assets under our management.

Every relationship begins with planning — but not every client needs a comprehensive plan. A project can be one decision or a family’s whole financial life, with a defined beginning, scope, and conclusion.

Often a single question

  • A rollover analysis, Social Security timing, a Roth conversion, a premarital package, or estate settlement.

We analyze, recommend, and hand you an implementation roadmap. When it concludes, implementation and monitoring generally return to you.

Consider a project when

  • You have a specific question to address.
  • You’re comfortable implementing recommendations yourself.
  • You want an independent fiduciary analysis without an ongoing relationship — and you’re always welcome to return.

Once you have an architecture, who keeps it current? With a project, that’s you. With Ongoing Fiduciary Planning, you delegate the bulk of it to us — on a defined planning calendar with milestones through the year.

What we take on

  • Proactively review planning issues and identify triggers.
  • Advance priorities and revisit strategies as things change.
  • Coordinate directly with your CPA and attorney.

Consider it when

  • You want someone other than yourself responsible for what happens next.
  • Your tax, estate, and retirement decisions frequently interact.
  • A five-year check-in isn’t enough for how often life changes.

Generally priced with an initial design-and-architecture fee plus a recurring advisory retainer — based on complexity and responsibility, not net worth alone.

Planning sets what an account is meant to accomplish; this puts that strategy into practice. When we manage assets, we assume fiduciary responsibility for:

  • Portfolio construction, allocation, and investment selection.
  • Monitoring, trading, and quarterly rebalancing.
  • Risk controls, cash and liquidity, and distributions.
  • Tax-aware management where applicable.

This is for the portfolio — not, by itself, ongoing monitoring of your full financial, tax, estate, or business planning (that’s Ongoing Fiduciary Planning). Where investment management isn’t the right fit, we can still plan around assets you manage elsewhere.

Most clients ultimately use more than one model, and many delegate both ongoing planning and investment management to us. None is inherently better — we price them separately so you only pay for the responsibilities you want us to hold.

Integrated Relationship Pricing

  • When we do both, statements, transfers, and third-party coordination fold into one relationship.
  • A separate planning fee coexists with a reduced asset-management rate, so the total reflects the actual combination of responsibilities.

The appropriate relationship depends upon what responsibilities you want to retain — and what responsibilities you want to delegate.
