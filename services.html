Every client relationship begins with some level of planning. From there, depending upon the work being performed, the client may elect—and Pointer Creek may agree—to provide ongoing fiduciary planning, ongoing fiduciary wealth management, or both.

This overview explains the differences among these service models, what responsibilities each places with Pointer Creek and the client, and circumstances in which one service model may be more appropriate than another.

At Pointer Creek Wealth Management, planning drives the advice.

Every relationship begins with understanding what your money is intended to accomplish. Before recommending an investment strategy, account structure, retirement distribution, estate strategy or other financial decision, we first seek to understand the purpose, circumstances and objectives behind it.

The amount of planning required depends upon the decision being made. For one client, that may mean a narrowly defined planning project addressing a single retirement account. For another, it may mean a comprehensive financial, retirement, tax and estate-planning engagement involving multiple generations, businesses, trust structures, properties and outside professionals.

Once the appropriate planning architecture has been established, you decide how you would like to proceed.

  • You may take the recommendations and implement and monitor them yourself.
  • You may retain Pointer Creek to continue managing and driving the planning process through Ongoing Fiduciary Planning.
  • You may retain Pointer Creek to manage investment assets in accordance with the objectives and strategy established through the planning process.
  • Or, where appropriate, you may engage Pointer Creek for both Ongoing Fiduciary Planning and Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management.

Planning establishes where you are going. The ongoing services you select determine what responsibilities you would like Pointer Creek to assume in helping you get there.

“What should we do?”

Fiduciary Planning Projects

Project planning answers a defined planning question or builds a defined planning architecture.

Every Pointer Creek relationship begins with planning, but that does not mean every client requires a comprehensive financial plan.

A Fiduciary Planning Project may be narrowly focused on a single decision or may encompass virtually every aspect of a family’s financial life. The scope and fee are established based upon the planning work actually required.

Examples may include:

  • Retirement and financial independence planning
  • Retirement-income and distribution planning
  • Estate and legacy planning
  • Investment and portfolio analysis
  • Retirement-plan or rollover analysis
  • Social Security and pension decisions
  • Tax-aware planning and Roth conversion analysis
  • Business-owner and succession planning
  • Risk-management and insurance analysis
  • Charitable planning
  • Trust and beneficiary planning
  • Family Limited Partnership analysis
  • Advanced estate-tax planning and strategy modeling
  • Premarital or family financial planning
  • A comprehensive financial, tax, retirement and estate-planning architecture

A planning project has a defined beginning, defined scope and defined conclusion. Pointer Creek analyzes the agreed-upon matters, develops recommendations and provides an implementation roadmap.

When the engagement concludes, responsibility for implementation, ongoing monitoring and recognizing when circumstances warrant another planning review generally returns to you.

For many organized, financially sophisticated clients who enjoy this type of work, this is exactly the “DIY with professional guidance” relationship they want.

Consider Project-Based Planning When:

  • You have a specific planning question or event to address.
  • You want comprehensive recommendations but are comfortable implementing them yourself.
  • You already have staff or systems responsible for ongoing implementation and monitoring.
  • Your circumstances are relatively stable and you prefer to return for planning assistance when you determine it is needed.
  • You want an independent fiduciary analysis without necessarily establishing an ongoing advisory relationship.
  • Your need is truly a one-time event, outside the scope of your expertise.

Examples might include; pre-marital plan package, divorce planning, estate settlement services, college funding analysis, etc.

Clients who disengage at the conclusion of a planning project are welcome to return for additional services when needed. Future updates, implementation assistance and new planning projects are available at then-current pricing.

“How does the necessary planning continue?”

Ongoing Fiduciary Planning

Ongoing Fiduciary Planning means Pointer Creek takes responsibility for keeping the planning process active.

When your initial planning project is completed, you have an architecture from which to work. The next question is who will be responsible for keeping it current?

With project-based planning, that responsibility generally returns to you.

With Ongoing Fiduciary Planning, you delegate the bulk of that responsibility to Pointer Creek.

Rather than waiting for you to recognize the next planning need, Pointer Creek proactively reviews applicable planning issues, identifies planning triggers, advances agreed-upon priorities, coordinates implementation and revisits strategies as circumstances change.

The fundamental difference is:

Who owns responsibility for keeping the planning process alive?

Depending upon the family and scope of work, our Ongoing Fiduciary Planning relationships may include Family & Private Enterprise Advisory Services, retirement and tax-planning coordination, estate and legacy oversight, or other ongoing planning arrangements.

What Ongoing Planning May Include

  • Recurring financial and family planning reviews
  • Annual tax and retirement-distribution planning
  • Ongoing Roth conversion and tax-bracket analysis
  • Regular Estate taxation, liquidity and beneficiary intent monitoring
  • Liquidity and spending coordination for major items that impact retirement or estate planning
  • Implementation tracking and scheduled follow-through
  • Coordination with attorneys, CPAs and other professionals
  • Family and generational planning as it appears or changes
  • Monitoring significant legislative and tax changes, modeling their impact to your plan goals and/or opportunities or challenges they may create
  • Event-driven planning when circumstances change
  • Basic planning, critical documents, and education for dependent family members where appropriate
  • Customized benefit election and coordination with changes to employment or employer benefit packages

Unlike a project engagement, there is no predetermined point at which Pointer Creek simply hands the planning responsibility back to you. Our responsibility to monitor, coordinate and adapt the agreed-upon planning strategies continues, on a defined planning calendar with multiple scheduled milestones throughout the year, for as long as you retain the service.

Consider Ongoing Fiduciary Planning When:

  • You want someone other than yourself responsible for remembering what needs to happen next.
  • Your planning includes strategies that should be evaluated annually or throughout the year.
  • Your tax, estate, retirement, family or business decisions frequently interact.
  • You want Pointer Creek coordinating with your CPA, attorney and other professionals towards your specific plan goals.
  • Your family circumstances or financial structure change frequently enough that a five-year planning event is unlikely to be sufficient.
  • You value proactive planning rather than returning only when you recognize a new issue yourself.

Ongoing Fiduciary Planning is generally priced using an initial design and architecture fee followed by a recurring advisory retainer. Pricing is based primarily upon planning complexity and the ongoing responsibilities accepted by Pointer Creek rather than net worth alone. Generally speaking, the initial design and architecture fee will be lower than a comparable one-time planning project, to offset the anticipated ongoing planning retainer.

“Who will manage the portfolio according to the plan?”

Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management

Fiduciary Wealth Management takes responsibility for managing the portfolio.

Planning establishes what an investment account is intended to accomplish. Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management puts that investment strategy into practice.

Before accepting assets for ongoing management, Pointer Creek first seeks to understand the purpose of the assets, expected time horizon, anticipated distributions, tax considerations, risk tolerance and capacity, and how the account fits within the client’s broader financial circumstances.

The amount of planning necessary may be very limited for a straightforward account or substantially more extensive for a complex household, trust(s), retirement plan(s) or institutional relationship.

When Pointer Creek accepts an investment-management engagement, we assume ongoing fiduciary responsibility for the assets entrusted to our management, which may include:

  • Portfolio construction and asset allocation
  • Investment selection and implementation
  • Ongoing monitoring of investments within the account
  • Trading and regular (quarterly) rebalancing of the account
  • Establishing risk management controls and monitoring
  • Cash and liquidity management
  • Portfolio-related distributions
  • Tax-aware investment management where applicable
  • Coordination among managed account types
  • Investment-policy development and monitoring

Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management is for the portfolio. It does not, by itself, mean Pointer Creek has accepted responsibility for continuously monitoring every aspect of the client’s financial, tax, estate, business or family planning. That broader responsibility belongs within an Ongoing Fiduciary Planning engagement discussed in the previous section.

Consider Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management When:

  • You want to delegate day-to-day portfolio-management, trading, rebalancing, investment selection and monitoring responsibility.
  • You want your investments implemented according to a defined planning purpose and investment strategy.
  • You prefer ongoing professional monitoring, rebalancing and implementation rather than managing the portfolio yourself.
  • Your assets and circumstances are appropriate for Pointer Creek’s investment-management platform and relationship requirements.

Not every planning client will be an appropriate fit for ongoing investment management. Pointer Creek evaluates investment-management engagements based upon the assets involved, required services, operational considerations and the economics necessary to responsibly maintain the relationship.

When ongoing investment management is not appropriate, Pointer Creek may still provide planning, investment analysis, allocation guidance and other project-based advice for assets the client manages directly or holds elsewhere.

Combining Our Services

One client may engage us solely for a planning project and then implement the recommendations independently.

Another client may complete a planning project and retain Pointer Creek to manage the resulting investment strategy.

Another may want Pointer Creek to continually oversee the planning process while trading their own investments in a self-directed brokerage account.

Most Pointer Creek clients ultimately use more than one service model and many families and organizations elect to delegate both their ongoing planning responsibilities and ongoing investment-management responsibilities to Pointer Creek.

None of these choices are inherently better than another. Pointer Creek has designed our pricing to be flexible and agnostic to the separate services you desire. Pointer Creek intentionally prices these services separately so clients can select the responsibilities they want us to assume without automatically paying for services they may not need. This advantage leaves us free to declare;

“The appropriate relationship depends upon what responsibilities you want to retain and what responsibilities you want to delegate.”

Integrated Relationship Pricing

Because each service is priced appropriately on a stand-alone basis, clients selecting more than one service may reasonably ask whether certain responsibilities overlap or become more efficient when performed together. We believe our pricing should recognize those efficiencies. When Pointer Creek provides both Ongoing Fiduciary Planning and Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management, certain activities become more efficient because they require less outside coordination to get things done.

When we directly manage an account, activities that might otherwise require outside statements, telephone calls, transfer instructions, third-party coordination and separate implementation can often be incorporated into the ongoing relationship.

Pointer Creek may therefore apply Integrated Relationship Pricing when clients engage us for both ongoing service models.

We believe a separate planning fee should appropriately coexist with a reduced asset-management rate so that the total relationship pricing reflects the combination of services being delivered.

The objective is straightforward: transparent pricing for the responsibilities Pointer Creek is actually being asked to assume.

A Simple Way to Think About It

“What should we do?”

Start with a Fiduciary Planning Project. Pointer Creek analyzes the situation, develops the architecture and provides the roadmap.

“How does the planning continue?”

Choose Ongoing Fiduciary Planning. Pointer Creek proactively monitors, coordinates & advances the planning process.

“Who will manage the portfolio according to the plan?”

Choose Ongoing Fiduciary Wealth Management. Pointer Creek assumes ongoing fiduciary responsibility for assets accepted under our management.

You may use one, two or all three service models depending upon your needs and the scope of the relationship we mutually agree to establish.

Planning comes first. From there, you decide what responsibilities you want to retain—and what responsibilities you would like to delegate.
